# `define_usd_camera`

Define a USD Camera at `path`. `clipping_range`, `camera_matrix`, `focal_length`, `focus_distance`, `horizontal_aperture`, `vertical_aperture` control the camera settings. `translation`, `rotation`, `scale` control the transform. 

`visibility` controls the visibility of the camera.
